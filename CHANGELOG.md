# Changelog

## [Unreleased] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- **Second jobs: Dark Knight (from Swordman) and Shaman (from Acolyte, female only).**
  - Both have full skill trees (13 + 15 skills, plus Two-Handed Sword Mastery for Swordman), adapted from Ragnarok Online DS concepts. See `docs/design/CLASS_DARK_KNIGHT_SHAMAN.md`.
  - The Job Guide offers second jobs at first-job Job Level 40.
  - Weapons and masteries are per class; two-handed weapons block shields.
- **Status effects and buffs:** Stun, Sleep, Stone, Silence, Blind and Confusion, with stat resistance, plus timed buffs, HP-drain buffs with a safety floor, and weapon element enchantments.
- **New weapons** at a Prontera Weapon Dealer: Katana, Claymore, Axe, Battle Axe, Club, Rod and Book.
- **Resource pack support (`Resources/`, loaded at runtime, never committed):**
  - Pack GRFs (CorrectedSprites, 2025 Palettes) override the client.
  - Loose sprite overlays, e.g. the Dark Knight body.
  - Sprite-sheet bodies, e.g. the Shaman, sliced and keyed at runtime.
  - The GRADIUS battle font.
- **Cloth dyes and extra hair colours** from palette packs, with steppers in character creation. Saves are now v5, adding aura, cloth dye and second jobs.
- **Damage numbers restyled:** battle font, blue gradient fill, white outline and soft glow.
  - Big hits and crits glow vibrant blue with a sheen.
  - Multi-hit skills show the individual hits, then build up to a big total.
- **Skill window:** one tab per job, branches in two columns, and full tooltips (SP, cast, delay, duration, range, weapons, prerequisites, locked state).
- **Badges** for Dark Knight and Shaman.
- **`Directive.md`:** a hand-off to-do list for AI agents.
- **Magic and elements:**
  - The Pre-Renewal element table (10 × 10 × 4) now applies to every attack. Magnum Break is fire.
  - MATK, MDEF and DEX-scaled cast times are in.
  - Moving or being hit interrupts a cast; SP is spent only when the spell goes off.
- **Mage skills:** Fire Bolt, Cold Bolt, Lightning Bolt, Napalm Beat (3×3 splash), Soul Strike (+Undead bonus) and Increase SP Recovery. Casters walk into range (9 cells) and then cast.
- **Element-coloured casting circles** (original procedural shaders):
  - Rotating rune band that lights up as the cast progresses, dot ring, light pillar and rising sparkles.
  - Each element has its own colour and motif: hexagram, crescent cross, flame petals, lightning star, octagram or wave rings.
  - The circle flashes on release, or collapses when interrupted.
- **Spell release effects:** bolts dropping from the sky (zig-zag for lightning), Soul Strike orbs, Napalm bursts, impact rings.
- **Cast bar** above the caster in the glass style, in the element's colour.
- **Player auras** (cosmetic): Class colours, Azure, Ember, Frost, Verdant, Storm, Radiant, Void, None.
  - Chosen at character creation and cycled in the field with **U**; saved with the character.
  - The aura dims while casting or sitting.

- **UI revision toward an anime-MMO system-window style** (SAO / PSO2 / Solo Leveling inspired, original design):
  - Translucent blue glass windows with a gradient title band, bright underline, ◆ title, inset frame and boxed close button.
  - SAO-style lime HP bars with a light stripe and silver rim.
  - The player panel rebuilt as an SAO party plate: badge, name, slanted Lv chip, HP bar with a thinner SP bar beneath, and a numbers plate.
  - PSO2-style stat bars in the status window.
- **SAO-style square class badges** for all 46 classes (`tools/insignia/generate_badges.gd`): class-family colour tiles with white glyphs, plus tier frames (white / double silver / icy blue / gold with a star). They're used everywhere the class icon appears; the heraldic shields remain available.
- **Job change** to all six first jobs (Swordman, Mage, Archer, Acolyte, Merchant, Thief) through the **Job Guide** in Prontera, with a glass NPC dialogue (requirements, choice, confirmation). Requires Novice job level 10 and Basic Skill 9.
  - The body sprite, insignia and status panel update.
  - A SYSTEM "JOB CHANGE" notice is shown and the game saves.
- **Class data** (`data/prerenewal/jobs.json`):
  - Pre-Renewal Max HP/SP per-class factors, class weight bonus and per-weapon attack delay (BTBA).
  - Class weapon restrictions.
  - The first-job job EXP table (job levels 1–50).
- **Swordman skills:**
  - Sword Mastery: +4 ATK per level with daggers and swords, added after DEF.
  - Increase HP Recovery: 10 s still regen and +10% healing items per level.
  - Bash: +30% ATK and +5% HIT per level; queued until the target is in range.
  - Magnum Break: hits every enemy within 2 cells, +20% ATK and +10% HIT per level; requires Bash 5. Skill-hit bursts are shown on screen.
- The skill window shows only the current class's skills, with prerequisite hints.
- Targeted skills on hotkeys aim at the monster under the cursor.
- **Camera near-fade:** geometry in the near part of the view dissolves, so tall buildings never wall off the screen.
- **Multiple maps and warps** (`data/prerenewal/maps.json`): per-map display names, respawn points, monster spawns and warp portals.
  - Prontera (town, with a Tool Dealer) connects to Prontera Field through the classic south gate; the portal cells were checked against the client's GAT walkability.
  - Walking onto a portal saves the character at the destination, shows a glass transition screen, and loads the new map.
  - Death respawns you at the map's respawn point.
- **Warp portal effect:** a swirling cyan ground sigil, a light column and a destination tag (original shaders).
- **Map title card** (SAO-style) shown on entering a map.
- **See-through circle:** models between the camera and the player are dithered away per pixel, so buildings never hide your character in towns.
- **Skills** (`data/prerenewal/skills.json`):
  - Basic Skill (1–9, learned with skill points; level 3 unlocks sitting).
  - First Aid (+5 HP, 3 SP, 1 s delay) and Play Dead (a toggle: monsters drop and ignore you; you can't move or attack). Both are quest skills, granted until their quests exist.
  - Job level-ups grant skill points.
- **Natural HP/SP regeneration:** Pre-Renewal amounts and intervals, twice as fast while sitting (Insert), and no regeneration at 50%+ weight or while playing dead.
- **Glass skill window** (K / Alt+S): client icons, level pips, learn buttons, tooltips with Basic Skill unlocks. Active skills can be dragged out.
- **Hotkey bar** (F1–F9, bottom centre): drag skills or usable items onto slots, use by key or click, right-click to clear, live item counts, and an "on" icon for active toggles. Saved per character.
- **Postures:** sitting and Play Dead are shown on the character.
- **Saves:** format v4 adds skills, skill points and hotkeys; older saves get skill points derived from job level.

### Changed
- Manual save moved to F12, because F1–F9 are now hotkeys.
- **Character select:** six local character slots in glass style, with a live rotating preview on a glowing platform, details (class insignia, levels, stats, location, Zeny), PLAY, and DELETE behind a confirmation.
- **Character creation:**
  - Name validation: 4–23 letters, numbers or single spaces, unique among local characters.
  - Sex, hairstyle (only styles the client actually has) and nine hair colours, previewed live with the client's sprites.
  - The classic Pre-Renewal stat hexagon as an interactive radar chart: opposing pairs sum to 10, and every build spends exactly 48 points.
  - New Novices start with a Knife and Cotton Shirt equipped and 3 Red Potions.
- **Title screen** rebuilt in glass over a live orbiting Prontera backdrop (`MenuBackdrop`). The flow is now Title → Character Select → Field (via the `GameSession` autoload).
- **Saves:** format v3 adds appearance (sex, hairstyle, hair colour); v1/v2 saves load with default looks. Characters are drawn with their saved appearance.
- **Equipment:** slots (weapon, shield, armor, garment, shoes, accessories, headgear), equip and unequip with swaps, and equipment weight counted toward the carry limit.
  - Pre-Renewal weapon damage: status ATK + weapon ATK with a DEX-based minimum, scaled by the weapon-vs-size table.
  - Armor adds hard DEF; Novice weapon delay (fist 0.5 s, dagger 0.65 s) feeds into ASPD.
  - The equipped weapon is drawn on the character using the client's weapon layer.
- **Items:** Knife, Main Gauche, Cotton Shirt, Guard, Hood and Sandals.
- **Merchant NPC** (`data/prerenewal/shops.json`): click to walk over and open the shop. Buy at the item price, sell at half price. Trades are validated by the simulation (Zeny, weight, distance), and walking away closes the shop.
- **Glass windows:** Equipment window (Q / Alt+Q) with per-slot rows, ATK/DEF/ASPD totals and double-click to unequip. Shop window with greeting, BUY/SELL tabs, item rows, quantity, total and Zeny. Double-click equipment in the inventory to equip it.
- **Saves:** format v2 adds equipment; v1 saves still load.
- **Items and loot:** item database (`data/prerenewal/items.json`) and Pre-Renewal drop tables for Poring, Fabre and Lunatic. Drops land around the death cell, the killer gets 3 s of exclusive pickup, and ground items expire after 60 s.
- **Inventory:** stacking, a 100-entry limit, and the Pre-Renewal weight limit (2000 + STR × 30). Pick up items by clicking them; use usable items to heal.
- **Glass windows:** draggable, with remembered positions.
  - Inventory (I / Alt+E): ITEM/EQUIP/ETC tabs, the client's own item icons, stack counts, rich tooltips, weight bar, Zeny; double-click to use.
  - Status (S / Alt+A): stat raising with Pre-Renewal costs, plus derived ATK/MATK/HIT/FLEE/CRIT/ASPD.
- **Feedback:** ground loot drawn with the client's item sprites, a drop hop and a glow ring; hover name tags; and a system message feed ("Obtained Jellopy ×1").
- **Save/load:** versioned local saves (`user://saves/slot1.json`) with field validation and clamping, atomic writes, autosave every 30 s, on level-up and on exit, and F5 to save manually.
- **Combat:** Pre-Renewal rules (hit/flee, critical, hard and soft DEF, ASPD) in `CombatRulesPreRenewal`; data-driven monsters (`data/prerenewal/monsters.json`) and EXP tables.
- **FieldSimulation:** deterministic and authoritative. Passive monster AI that retaliates, attack timing, damage-share EXP, base/job level-ups and respawns, all emitted as an event stream for presentation.
- **Twilight Glass UI:** frosted glass panels (backdrop blur, chamfered corners, hairline border, corner brackets, light sweep) and slanted glow bars whose HP colour shifts green, amber, red, with a lingering white damage chip.
  - Top-left status panel: fades when idle, wakes on HP/SP changes, collapsible and remembered.
  - Target frame, name-tagged world HP bars shown from first engagement until death, condensed glowing damage numbers, and a SYSTEM level-up notification.
- **Class insignia:** original generator (`tools/insignia`: emblem painter plus a shared render shader) producing 46 rank-consistent 512 px PNGs (wood, bronze, silver, wood/bronze with icy trim, gold). The insignia is shown beside the class title.
- **Sprites:** ACT layer tint and alpha applied in sprite composition, which fixes effects such as the Poring death splash.
- Walkable character: `GridPathfinder` (8-directional A* over GAT, no corner cutting, 64-step limit) and `GridWalker` (deterministic 150 ms/cell movement, 1.4× for diagonals, re-click finishes the current step). Both are pure gameplay logic.
- `CharacterSprite`: body and head composed through ACT anchors with hair palettes, shown as a camera-facing billboard that is depth-tested against the map.
- `PlayerAvatar` and the "Explore Prontera" offline prototype scene: click-to-walk, follow camera, target marker.
- Movement unit tests and a visual direction-ring capture.
- RSM/RSM2 model decoder (1.4, 1.5, 2.2, 2.3) and mesh baker. Maps now render their buildings, trees and props (for example, 1,303 instances in Prontera).
- Model shader with classic ambient/diffuse lighting. Synthetic RSM unit test and sampled client model test.

### Fixed
- Shop catalogue IDs parsed from JSON as floats never matched integer item IDs, so nothing could be bought; they are now normalised to integers.
- RSW 2.7 model records: the extra f32 precedes the filename, so RSM2 references on 2.7 maps now resolve.

### Earlier
- GAT, GND (1.7/1.8/1.9) and RSW (1.9 to 2.7) decoders. All 3,366 map files in the reference client parse completely.
- Ground renderer: per-texture mesh surfaces, lightmap atlas, smoothed normals, classic sRGB lighting shader, and a water plane.
- `RoMap` node with cell/world conversion, `RoCamera` isometric camera, and a Map Viewer tool (added to the main menu).
- BMP/TGA/JPG/PNG texture loader with magenta colour keying.
- Map unit tests (synthetic fixtures) and an all-maps client integration test.
- Environment and repository audit (`PROJECT_AUDIT.md`).
- Godot 4.8 project in `06_Godot_Project`.
- GRF archive reader supporting versions 0x200 and 0x300, with a CP949 filename codec.
- SPR and ACT decoders, plus the `ActSprite` animated layer renderer.
- `RoResources` read-only virtual file system: loose files first, then GRFs in DATA.INI order.
- Main menu with client-folder mounting, and a Sprite Viewer developer tool.
- Pre-Renewal stat rules (status points, raise cost, HIT/FLEE, crit, status ATK, MATK).
- Private co-op `InvitationCode` (48-bit secret, CRC-16, versioned).
- Headless test runner: 16 unit tests and 5 client integration tests.
- Allow-list `.gitignore` so no game data reaches the public repository.
